# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Project discussions, issues, reviews and contributions should be respectful, evidence-based and welcoming. Critique proposals, code and claims rather than a person's identity or motives. Explain disagreements with reproducible examples, distinguish fact from uncertainty, acknowledge mistakes and give others space to participate. Respect privacy and applicable contribution/security boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained personal attacks, intimidation and deliberate disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruption, impersonation, knowingly fabricated test/security claims or retaliation against a reporter are also unacceptable.

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces and to conduct while representing this project. It does not appoint a standards body, certify participants or imply authority over unrelated communities. Use of AI assistance does not remove responsibility for respectful conduct, attribution, accuracy or review.

## Reporting

The initial accountable maintainer is [@kunal-y-abi](https://github.com/kunal-y-abi), the verified repository-owner account identified in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Request a private contact from that maintainer through a [non-sensitive repository issue](https://github.com/kunal-y-abi/aps-dda/issues), without personal allegations or identifying details, and verify the recipient/channel before sending a conduct report. **Do not use GitHub security advisories for ordinary conduct reports.** No email address, response deadline or staffed enforcement team is declared.

Through the verified private contact, report relevant dates/context, safe links or excerpts and the requested privacy considerations. Do not publish personal information or sensitive allegations in an issue. If no private route is available, request one from the verified repository owner with a non-sensitive message and confirm it before sharing details. A vulnerability belongs in the process described by [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); urgent personal-safety concerns should use appropriate external assistance rather than assume the repository can provide emergency support.

## Intended enforcement process

Accountable maintainers should consider reports fairly, protect private information to the extent possible, avoid retaliation and disclose only what is necessary to address the conduct. A maintainer with a conflict of interest should not decide their own case. Absolute confidentiality is not promised, including where an applicable safety or legal duty requires disclosure.

Responses may include clarification, a request to stop, content moderation, a warning, a temporary participation restriction or removal from project spaces, proportionate to severity and repetition. Decisions should document the behavior and rationale without unnecessarily exposing reporter details. Appeals should be reviewed by an uninvolved accountable maintainer where one is available; the initial single-maintainer roster does not provide an independent appeals panel.

This is project-specific conduct guidance, not a legal determination or emergency-support service.
